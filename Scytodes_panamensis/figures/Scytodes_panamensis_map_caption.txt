Figure. Exploratory climatic suitability for Scytodes panamensis in Panama and northern South America. Colours show the continuous cloglog output of the LQ model with regularization multiplier 1.5; the displayed colour scale spans 0.57–0.81 (raster range 0.5758–0.8010). Symbols represent four unique Panamanian GBIF localities and 13 new localities reported in the manuscript (seven Colombian and six tentatively identified Venezuelan localities). Nearby symbols overlap. These 17 coordinate localities were reduced to 14 occupied environmental cells for model fitting. Grey land indicates areas without predictions, not unsuitable habitat. Mean leave-one-out validation AUC was 0.582 ± 0.152 SD. Constant candidates had lower AICc overall; predictions should therefore be interpreted as exploratory hypotheses for future surveys. Country boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m; climate: WorldClim 2.5 arc-minutes. GBIF occurrence download: https://doi.org/10.15468/dl.p33n36.
